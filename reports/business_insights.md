# European E-Commerce Analytics
## Business Insights and Recommendations

### 1. Sales Performance
- Original valid-sale gross sales: £20,972,594.55
- Deduplicated gross sales: £20,913,891.45
- Deduplicated orders: 40,077
- Unique customers: 5,878
- Deduplicated average order value: £521.84

### 2. Customer Segmentation
- Six customer segments were identified using RFM analysis.
- Champions contributed 68.17% of customer-linked gross sales.
- Customer concentration suggests the importance of retaining
  high-value customers and developing other segments.

### 3. Customer Retention
- Month 1 weighted retention: 23.15%
- Month 2 weighted retention: 23.27%
- Month 3 weighted retention: 24.51%
- Month 6 weighted retention: 21.78%

Recommendation:
Investigate repeat-purchase opportunities and customer
engagement strategies to improve retention.

### 4. Data Quality
- Exact duplicate rows identified: 12,133
- Duplicate valid-sale rows: 12,061
- Duplicate-associated valid-sale gross sales: £58,703.10
- Duplicate revenue share: approximately 0.28%

Recommendation:
Implement duplicate detection and reconciliation checks
before publishing financial KPIs.

### 5. Analytical Limitations
- The dataset covers historical transactions from 2009–2011.
- The customer segmentation and cohort retention results
  require verification against the deduplicated dataset.
- Recommendations are analytical hypotheses, not measured
  outcomes of implemented business interventions.
